Use in this project: appearance only (render_scene.py). The simulation in net_sim.py does
not read these files. Do not take physics from cf21B_500.xml: its mass (0.04338 kg) is
Crazyflow's own configuration and its diaginertia (2.3951e-5, 2.3951e-5, 3.2347e-5) is
the brushed Crazyflie 2 value, not the identified Brushless inertia.

Geometry stated in the XML: motor sites at (+-35.36 mm, +-35.36 mm, 0) in the order
motor0 (+x,-y), motor1 (-x,-y), motor2 (-x,+y), motor3 (+x,+y); propeller bodies 12 mm
above the body origin; inertial (centre of mass) at the body origin.
